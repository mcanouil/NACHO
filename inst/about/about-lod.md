The limit of detection (**LoD**) comes from the detection of **POS_E**, the `0.5 fM` positive control probe.
This probe corresponds to about 10,000 copies of the target in each sample tube.

On a **FLEX**/**MAX** system, the standard input of `100 ng` of total RNA corresponds to about 10,000 cell equivalents, assuming that one cell holds `10 pg` of total RNA on average.
An assay on a **FLEX**/**MAX** system should therefore detect about one transcript copy per cell for each target, or 10,000 transcript copies in total.
In most codeset assays, even the **POS_F** probe, which is equivalent to 0.25 copies per cell, is detectable above background.
