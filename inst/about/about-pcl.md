Six synthetic DNA control targets come with every nCounter Gene Expression assay.
In the codeset, their concentrations range linearly from `128 fM` to `0.125 fM`, and they are named **POS_A** to **POS_F**.
These **Positive Controls** measure the efficiency of the hybridization reaction.
Their step-wise concentrations also let you check the linearity of the assay.

The concentrations of the **Positive Controls** increase in a linear fashion, so the counts should do the same.
The nSolver preset of NACHO computes the **Positive Control Linearity** (**PCL**) from **POS_A** to **POS_E**.
The legacy preset also uses **POS_F**.
